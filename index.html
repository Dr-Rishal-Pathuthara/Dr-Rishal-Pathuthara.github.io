Introduction

About Me

I am a PhD Scholar at the Faculty of Dental Medicine and Oral Health Sciences, McGill University. My academic qualifications include a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and a Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Oral Pathology and Microbiology.

My research interests include artificial intelligence in healthcare, oral lesion assessment, early oral cancer detection and evidence-informed clinical decision support. I am interested in how multimodal AI, privacy-preserving learning and structured clinical information may contribute to clinically useful research tools.

Research Portfolio

Research Projects

My research interests span AI-assisted oral lesion assessment, privacy-aware model development and the translation of clinical evidence into practice.

PROJECT 01

Federated Multimodal AI for Oral Cancer Detection

Investigating privacy-preserving multimodal artificial intelligence approaches for early oral cancer detection by integrating oral lesion images with relevant clinical information across participating institutions.

Federated Learning Multimodal AI Oral Cancer
PROJECT 02

Lesion-Aware Conversational AI for Clinical History Taking

Exploring an AI-assisted workflow that generates relevant follow-up questions based on the type of oral lesion and available clinical information. The resulting history could be combined with lesion images to support a more informed clinical assessment.

Conversational AI Clinical Reasoning Image + History
PROJECT 03

Evidence-Informed AI Implementation for Oral Lesion Management

Developing an implementation framework for AI-assisted oral lesion detection, risk stratification and evidence-linked management recommendations for clinician review, including appropriate referral and further investigation.

Implementation Science Clinical Decision Support Evidence Synthesis
