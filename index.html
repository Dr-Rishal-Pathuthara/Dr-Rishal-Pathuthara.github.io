Introduction

About Me

I am a PhD Scholar at the Faculty of Dental Medicine and Oral Health Sciences, McGill University. My academic background includes a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and a Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Oral Pathology and Microbiology.

This website is my professional space to share my academic journey, research interests, publications, projects and ideas. I am particularly interested in exploring the potential of artificial intelligence in healthcare.
