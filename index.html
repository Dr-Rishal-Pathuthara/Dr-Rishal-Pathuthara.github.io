About Me

Clinical Foundations.
Research-Driven Innovation.

I am a PhD Scholar at McGill University's Faculty of Dental Medicine and Oral Health Sciences, with academic training in dentistry and oral pathology and microbiology.

My research interests include artificial intelligence in oral medicine and diagnostics, oral pathology and oral cancer research, public health dentistry, evidence synthesis, and digital health.

My previous research experience includes work in artificial intelligence in oral medicine at Qatar University's College of Dental Medicine, including clinical data annotation, evidence synthesis, AI validation and educational platforms, and research collaboration.

I am interested in connecting clinical needs with rigorous evidence, responsible AI development, and approaches that can be evaluated for their relevance to real-world dental practice.

Research Portfolio

Research Directions

Proposed areas of investigation at the intersection of oral pathology, artificial intelligence, and clinical practice.

01

Federated Multimodal AI for Oral Cancer Detection

Exploring privacy-preserving AI approaches that combine oral lesion images with relevant clinical information. Federated learning may support investigation of model development across institutions while data remain at their source, subject to privacy and governance safeguards.

AI · Oral Oncology · Privacy
02

Lesion-Aware Conversational AI

Exploring conversational systems that adapt clinical history questions to the characteristics of an oral lesion and combine collected history with image-based assessment to support structured information gathering by dental professionals.

Conversational AI · Clinical Assessment
03

Evidence-Informed AI for Oral Lesion Management

Exploring how clinical evidence and practice guidelines could inform AI-assisted oral lesion assessment, risk stratification, and management support, including referral and biopsy considerations, clinical validation, and clinician oversight.

Evidence Synthesis · Clinical Decision Support

These entries describe research interests and proposed directions, not claims that the systems have been clinically validated or implemented.
