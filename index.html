Introduction

About Me

I am an oral pathologist and researcher with academic and clinical experience in dentistry. My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, oral medicine, oral pathology, cancer research, and public health.

I am particularly interested in developing and evaluating AI-based approaches for oral lesion analysis, oral cancer screening, clinical decision support, and digital health applications. I also work in evidence synthesis, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, and population-based health research.

My research approach emphasizes methodological rigor, interdisciplinary collaboration, clinical relevance, and the responsible translation of emerging technologies into healthcare.

Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the College of Dental Medicine, Qatar University, contributing to projects involving AI in oral medicine, evidence synthesis, and digital health.
