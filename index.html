About Me

Clinical Foundations.
Research-Driven Innovation.

I am a PhD Scholar at McGill University's Faculty of Dental Medicine and Oral Health Sciences, with academic training in dentistry and oral pathology and microbiology.

My research interests focus on the potential role of artificial intelligence in oral healthcare, including oral lesion assessment, early oral cancer detection, clinical history taking, and evidence-informed decision support.

I am particularly interested in connecting clinical needs with rigorous evidence, responsible AI development, and approaches that can be evaluated for their relevance to real-world dental practice.

Research Portfolio

Areas of Research Interest

Research directions at the intersection of oral medicine, artificial intelligence, and evidence-informed clinical practice.

01

Federated Multimodal AI for Oral Cancer Detection

Exploring privacy-preserving AI approaches that combine oral lesion images with relevant clinical information. Federated learning may offer a way to investigate model development across institutions while keeping data at its source, subject to appropriate privacy and governance safeguards.

AI · Oral Oncology · Privacy
02

Lesion-Aware Conversational AI

Exploring conversational systems that adapt clinical history questions to the characteristics of an oral lesion and combine the collected history with image-based assessment. The aim is to investigate whether this could support more structured information gathering by dental professionals.

Conversational AI · Clinical Assessment
03

Evidence-Informed AI for Oral Lesion Management

Exploring how clinical evidence and practice guidelines could inform AI-assisted oral lesion assessment, risk stratification, and management support. Potential areas include referral and biopsy considerations, evidence mapping, clinical validation, and appropriate clinician oversight.

Evidence Synthesis · Clinical Decision Support
